Privacy Policy

Welcome to Magiban Technologies Pvt Ltd! We value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our software services and mobile products.

Effective Date: December 10, 2024 | Updated: August 2026

Applies to our apps

1. Introduction

Welcome to Magiban Technologies Pvt Ltd! We value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our software services and products. By using our services, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use our software:

  • Personal Information: Details such as your name, email address, phone number, and contact details you provide.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our software, including usage patterns, errors, and system data.
  • Device Information: Information about the devices you use to access our services, such as IP addresses, operating systems, and device types.

3. Health Data Collection & Usage

For applications that provide health, fitness, or habit tracking features (such as Solo Level Up: System Fitness and Habit Tracker: Atmos Sphere), we access and collect health-related data. This section comprehensively discloses our practices regarding Health data:

  • Collection & Access: We collect health and fitness related data such as physical activity, exercise routines, step counts, calories burned, heart rate, or related measurements. This data is accessed only with your explicit permission through device sensors (e.g., Google Health Connect, Apple HealthKit) or via your direct manual input.
  • Usage: The health data collected is used strictly to power the core functionality of our health and fitness features. We use this data to track your progress, deliver personalized insights, and improve your overall wellness experience within our application.
  • Data Privacy & Security: Your health data is treated as highly sensitive personal information. We DO NOT share, sell, rent, or trade your health data with ad networks, data brokers, or other third parties.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our services
  • To improve the functionality and performance of our software
  • To communicate with you about updates, promotions, and other relevant information
  • To monitor and analyze usage trends and performance issues
  • To comply with legal obligations

5. Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously. We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of data transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  • When required by law or to comply with legal processes
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, and that of our users
  • With service providers who assist us in operating our services under confidentiality agreements

7. Your Rights

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • The right to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • The right to delete your personal data, subject to certain legal restrictions
  • The right to withdraw your consent to data processing at any time

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

9. Children's Privacy & Families Policy Compliance

We take children's privacy with utmost seriousness. For our child-directed educational applications, specifically Kiddoz: Early Learning & Stories (com.magiban.org.kiddoz), we strictly comply with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) from children.
  • No Ad Tracking or Identifiers: In accordance with Google Play Families Policy, our children's apps do not transmit Advertising IDs (AAID), device identifiers, or telemetric data to third parties.
  • 100% On-Device Operation: Story progress, audio playback, drawing canvases, and tracing metrics are processed entirely offline on the user's local device.

For our other general-audience services, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated effective date.
