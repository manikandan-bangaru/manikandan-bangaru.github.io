QuestFit: Workout & Diet RPG

  • Apple Health: open Profile > Settings > Health Settings and turn on the data you want to sync. Each switch asks only for its own data. You can change access any time in the Health app > Sharing > Apps.
  • Your data: there is no account. Your progress is stored on your iPhone; deleting the app deletes it.
  • Premium: Profile > Settings > Remove Ads. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, use Restore Purchases on the same screen. To cancel a subscription, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone.
  • Reminders: Profile > Settings > Notification Settings.
  • Report an ad: Profile > Settings > Report an ad.
